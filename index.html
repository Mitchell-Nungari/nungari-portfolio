01 / Intro

Pro · Design, programming & control

Intro

I'm Mitchell Nungari, a lighting designer and programmer for live events and productions, based in Nairobi.

For ten years I have designed and programmed lighting for clubs, concerts, festivals, theatre, broadcast and corporate shows — from weekly resident rigs to stages with more than 300 fixtures and crowds in the thousands.

I work on grandMA2, grandMA3, Avolites and HOG4, and handle the technical side of a production: light plots and patch lists, DMX and Art-Net networking, cue programming and busking, 3D pre-visualisation, load calculations, rigging and trussing, plus fixture repair and on-site safety. See my work, or get in touch about your next event.
